Privacy policy

HourglassPost is built to know as little about you as possible. Here's exactly what happens to your data.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

The short version

  • HourglassPost has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no trackers of its own.
  • We don't run a server or database, so we don't collect or store your messages or any personal information.
  • Your mailbox is provided by Guerrilla Mail, under its own terms.
  • Temporary inboxes are public and messages are deleted after about one hour.

Who we are

HourglassPost is an independent free tool, published as a static website on GitHub Pages. See the About page.

What is stored in your browser

  • sessionStorage (this tab only): the Guerrilla Mail session token, your current temporary address and the time it was created. It lets a page reload keep the same inbox. Your browser deletes it when you close the tab.
  • localStorage: only your light/dark theme choice, if you change it.

Neither is a cookie, and neither is sent to us. You can clear both at any time through your browser's site-data settings.

The mail provider: Guerrilla Mail

To create your address and fetch your messages, your browser connects directly to Guerrilla Mail's public API (api.guerrillamail.com). HourglassPost is not affiliated with Guerrilla Mail. Like any website, Guerrilla Mail receives technical information such as your IP address and your browser's user-agent string, and it receives and stores the emails sent to your temporary address. Its handling of that data is governed by the Guerrilla Mail Terms of Service. Requests are sent without cookies.

Your inbox is public

A temporary inbox has no password. Anyone who knows or guesses the address can read its mail. Please don't use HourglassPost for anything personal, financial or confidential.

How long data lasts

Guerrilla Mail deletes messages after about one hour. You can also delete a message yourself while reading it. The session in your browser ends when you close the tab.

Hosting

The website files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data, such as IP addresses, in its server logs for security and operations, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. HourglassPost does not receive or use those logs.

Emails you open

Messages are shown in a sandbox with scripts and remote images blocked, so senders can't use tracking pixels through HourglassPost. If you click a link inside an email, the website you visit has its own privacy practices.

Children

HourglassPost is a general-purpose tool and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we add something that changes this policy (for example, optional support or analytics), we'll update this page and the date above first.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
