    What is a temporary email?
    
      
    
        
    
          
    A temporary email (also called a disposable or throw-away address) is a real, working inbox that you use once and then let go. Many websites ask for an email address before you can download a file, read an article, join a Wi-Fi hotspot or start a free trial. Giving them your personal address often means months of newsletters and marketing mail you never asked for.
    
          
    With HourglassPost you paste a random address instead, receive the confirmation link or code here, and move on. After about an hour the messages are deleted automatically, so the address can't fill up with spam later and your real inbox never hears from that site.
    
          
    The trade-off is that a temporary inbox is public and short-lived. It's perfect for low-stakes sign-ups, but the wrong choice for anything you'll need to log back into. Read the FAQ or see how it works.
